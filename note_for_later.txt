Future Improvement: Report Effect Sizes

Purpose: - Statistical significance (p-values) tells whether a
difference is likely real (not due to random chance). - Effect size
tells how large or practically important that difference is.

Why add it? - A result can be statistically significant but have a very
small practical impact. - Effect sizes help readers judge whether the
findings are meaningful for business decisions.

Recommended Effect Sizes: - Kruskal–Wallis test → Epsilon squared (ε²) -
Measures how much variation is explained by the grouping variable. -
Dunn’s post-hoc test → Cliff’s Delta or Rank-biserial Correlation -
Measures the magnitude of pairwise differences between groups. - (For
future parametric analyses) - ANOVA → Eta squared (η²) or Partial Eta
squared

Keywords to search later: - Statistical significance vs practical
significance - Effect size - Epsilon squared (Kruskal–Wallis) - Cliff’s
Delta - Rank-biserial Correlation - Eta squared (ANOVA)
