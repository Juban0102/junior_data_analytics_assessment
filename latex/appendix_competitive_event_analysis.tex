\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
	
	\item Competitive events were associated with a statistically significant decrease in ROAS.
	
	\item Campaigns conducted during competitive events generated significantly higher CTR.
	
	\item No statistically significant difference in CVR was observed between competitive and non-competitive periods.
	
	\item CPC increased significantly during competitive events, indicating greater advertising costs to acquire user clicks.
	
	\item Collectively, these findings suggest that reduced profitability during competitive events is primarily driven by higher acquisition costs rather than changes in customers' likelihood of purchasing after clicking an advertisement.
	
\end{itemize}
